Operating mode

Monitoring-only mode is configured on the Raspberry Pi with BASELINE_MODE. Relay requests are enforced by the device agent. An authorized RFID card must be scanned twice within 5 seconds to clear a trip latch; reset leaves all outputs OFF.

Support

Operating notes

  1. Confirm sensor diagnostics match each physical circuit.
  2. Each circuit’s Wh budget is based on accumulated daily meter energy and resets at midnight.
  3. The total system cap remains an instantaneous W cutoff.
  4. The Pi rejects ON requests on sensor or configuration failure. Verify relay polarity and wiring with a safe test setup.
